⭐ Mexican Chicken & Rice — the written recipe

Written down so nobody has to scrub through a video with chicken hands. The creator's recipe, doubled to make our 10 portions.

450 cal · 45P · 47C · 9F per portion

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 2 value packs (7 lb total across both dishes; about half goes in here)
  • Dry rice — about 2¼ cups (we used 2 cups our first time and it felt like too little rice — don't short it)
  • Black beans — 1 can (15 oz), drained & rinsed
  • Canned diced tomatoes — 2 cans (14.5 oz each) — our addition from the video; not in the creator's written recipe
  • Yellow onions — 2, diced
  • Red bell peppers — 2, diced
  • Limes — 2 (juice + wedges)
  • Fresh coriander / cilantro — chopped, for garnish
  • Chicken seasoning: 4 tsp oregano, 4 tsp paprika, 2 tsp each of garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, salt & black pepper
  • Olive oil — 4 tsp, for cooking
  • About 2½ cups water or chicken stock (the tomato juice counts as liquid)

Method

  1. Cube the chicken and season it all over with the chicken seasoning above (4 tsp oregano, 4 tsp paprika, 2 tsp each of the rest) and the juice of 2 limes. Rest 10 minutes while you chop the onion and peppers.
  2. Cook the chicken in a little olive oil in a big pot or deep pan, 6–8 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onion and bell pepper until soft.
  4. Add the dry rice, 2 tsp of each seasoning (the same mix as the chicken), and the diced tomatoes — toast 1–2 minutes, stirring.
  5. Add the water/stock and the black beans. Mix well, cover, and simmer on low 12–15 minutes, stirring halfway so the rice doesn't stick.
  6. Fold the chicken back in with the chopped coriander and a final squeeze of lime. Divide into 10 containers.
⚖️ Portioning — how a serving stays 450 cal

The whole batch is about 4,500 cal, so one true portion is exactly one tenth of the pot. Portioning is what makes the 450 real:

  • Stir the finished dish really well first — if the chicken settles, one container ends up all rice and another all chicken, and the calories drift even if they look the same size.
  • With a kitchen scale: weigh the whole batch, divide by 10, fill each container to that weight.
  • No scale: line up all 10 containers and fill them in rounds — one scoop in each, repeat — then level them off, moving a spoonful from the fullest to the lightest.
  • Short on containers? Five per dish works — each holds 2 portions (a day for both of us), split in half when serving.
  • Storage: 6 containers per dish in the fridge (Mon–Wed), 4 in the freezer (Thu–Fri). Move frozen ones to the fridge the night before.
