⭐ Mexican Chicken & Rice — makes 10 portions

450 cal · 45P · 47C · 9F per portion

Ingredients & method — tap to open

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 1,875 g (about 4.1 lb)
  • Dry rice — 525 g (about 2¾ cups)
  • Black beans — 1 can (250 g), drained & rinsed
  • Canned diced tomatoes — 2 cans (14.5 oz each)
  • Yellow onions — 3, diced
  • Red bell peppers — 3, diced
  • Limes — 3 (juice + wedges)
  • Fresh coriander / cilantro — chopped, for garnish
  • Seasoning (chicken, then again for the rice): oregano, garlic powder, paprika, cumin, chili powder, salt, black pepper
  • About 3 cups water or chicken stock (the tomato juice counts as liquid)

Method

  1. Cube the chicken and season it all over with the oregano, garlic powder, paprika, cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper, and the juice of 1 lime. Rest 10 minutes while you chop the onion and peppers.
  2. Cook the chicken in a little olive oil in a big pot or deep pan, 6–8 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onion and bell pepper until soft.
  4. Add the dry rice, 1 tsp of each seasoning, and the diced tomatoes — toast 1–2 minutes, stirring.
  5. Add the water/stock and the black beans. Mix well, cover, and simmer on low 12–15 minutes, stirring halfway so the rice doesn't stick.
  6. Fold the chicken back in with the chopped coriander and a final squeeze of lime. Divide into 10 containers.

Teriyaki Chicken Fried Rice — makes 10 portions

450 cal · 45P · 36C · 12F per portion

Ingredients & method — tap to open

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 60 oz (3.75 lb), diced
  • Cooked rice — 40 oz (cook about 380 g dry, the day before if you can; chilled rice works best)
  • Eggs — 6
  • Frozen mixed vegetables — 150 g (about ½ a bag)
  • On the chicken in the cooker: garlic salt, black pepper, chili crisp
  • Teriyaki sauce: soy sauce, rice vinegar, sriracha, minced garlic, honey
  • On the rice: soy sauce + sesame oil
  • Yum yum sauce: light mayo, ketchup, rice vinegar, garlic salt, sugar, smoked paprika, onion powder, black pepper
  • Optional garnish: sesame seeds + chopped green onions

Method — everything finishes in the cooker, like the video

  1. Slow cooker (as in the video): diced chicken + garlic salt, black pepper, and chili crisp in the slow cooker. Cook on High about 2 hours.
  2. Instant Pot, Slow Cook mode: same as above — More = High, steam vent OPEN, about 2–2½ hours.
  3. Instant Pot, pressure shortcut: chicken + seasonings, High Pressure about 8 minutes, then release. Check the chicken hits 165°F.
  4. While the chicken cooks: whisk the yum yum sauce, cook the rice, and season the cooked rice with the soy sauce and sesame oil.
  5. Stir the teriyaki sauce into the cooked chicken. Scramble the 6 eggs in a pan.
  6. Add the seasoned rice, frozen vegetables, and scrambled eggs to the cooker. Stir everything together, cover, and cook until hot through (about 30 minutes on Slow Cook, or a few minutes on Sauté / Keep Warm for the pressure version).
  7. Divide into 10 containers, drizzle yum yum sauce over each, and top with sesame seeds and green onions if using.
Instant Pot note: we don't own a separate slow cooker — the Instant Pot covers both jobs. Slow Cook mode for the true set-it-and-forget-it version, pressure mode when prep day is running long. Same dish either way.
