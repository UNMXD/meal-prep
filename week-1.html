Mexican Chicken & Rice — makes 10 portions

450 cal · 45P · 47C · 9F per portion

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 1,875 g (about 4.1 lb)
  • Dry basmati rice — 525 g (about 2¾ cups)
  • Black beans — 1 can (250 g), drained & rinsed
  • Yellow onions — 3, diced
  • Red bell peppers — 3, diced
  • Limes — 3 (juice + wedges)
  • Seasoning: garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, salt, black pepper
  • About 4½ cups water or chicken stock, for the rice

Method

  1. Season the chicken all over with the spices, salt, and a squeeze of lime juice. Rest 10 minutes while you chop the onion and peppers.
  2. Sear the chicken in a big pot or deep pan until browned and cooked through. Set aside to rest, then slice or dice it.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onion and bell pepper until soft.
  4. Add the dry rice and stir for 1 minute to toast it. Add the water/stock and the black beans.
  5. Cover and simmer on low about 12–15 minutes, until the rice is done. Rest 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff.
  6. Fold the chicken back in with a final squeeze of lime. Divide into 10 containers.

Teriyaki Chicken Fried Rice — makes 10 portions

450 cal · 45P · 36C · 12F per portion

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 60 oz (3.75 lb)
  • Cooked rice — 40 oz (cook about 380 g dry; chilled rice fries best)
  • Eggs — 6
  • Frozen mixed vegetables — 2 bags
  • Teriyaki sauce (from home): soy sauce, honey, sriracha, garlic powder, sesame oil
  • Yum yum sauce: light mayo + sriracha + a little honey, thinned with a splash of water

Method — the chicken, 3 ways (pick one)

  1. Slow cooker (as in the video): chicken + teriyaki sauce in the slow cooker — Low about 5–6 hours or High about 3 hours, until it shreds easily with a fork. Shred it and toss it back through the sauce.
  2. Instant Pot, Slow Cook mode: same as above — Normal = Low, More = High, steam vent OPEN, and give it a little extra time (add roughly 30–60 minutes). Shred when it pulls apart easily.
  3. Instant Pot, pressure shortcut: chicken + teriyaki sauce, High Pressure 12 minutes, then 10 minutes natural release. Check the chicken hits 165°F, then shred.

Method — building the fried rice

  1. Scramble the 6 eggs in a big pan or wok; set aside.
  2. In the same pan, stir-fry the frozen vegetables until hot.
  3. Add the cooked rice and fry until it picks up a little color.
  4. Fold in the shredded teriyaki chicken (with some of its sauce) and the eggs.
  5. Divide into 10 containers and drizzle yum yum sauce over each.
Instant Pot note: we don't own a separate slow cooker — the Instant Pot covers both jobs. Slow Cook mode for the true set-it-and-forget-it version, pressure mode when prep day is running long. Same dish either way.
