🌯 Enchilada Bowls — the written recipe

The creator's batch makes 7 portions; this is it scaled up to our 10.

480 cal · 51P · 44C · 10F per portion

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — about 4.3 lb, diced
  • Dry jasmine rice — about 2¼ cups
  • Kraft American Singles — 14 to 15 slices (one 16 ct pack)
  • Bell peppers — 5, chopped
  • White onions — 2, chopped
  • Old El Paso red enchilada sauce — 3 cans (10 oz each)
  • Seasoning for the chicken: garlic powder about 1½ Tbsp, paprika about 1 Tbsp, salt about 1½ tsp, black pepper about 1½ tsp

Method

  1. Season the diced chicken with the garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook the chicken with the chopped peppers and onion in a big pan until the chicken hits 165°F.
  3. Cook the rice separately (rice cooker: rice + 3 cups water, scaled a touch for our bigger batch).
  4. Stir the cooked rice, the cheese slices and all 3 cans of sauce into the chicken pan.
  5. Simmer about 5 minutes until the cheese melts through. Divide into 10 containers.
🍗 KFC Famous Bowls — the written recipe (our version)

Makes 10 bowls. Per bowl: ½ cup mash base, ¾ cup corn, 4 oz chicken, 1 Tbsp cheddar, 2 Tbsp gravy.

About 487 cal · about 49P per bowl (estimated)

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — about 3.3 lb raw (cooks down to 4 oz per bowl), seasoned your way
  • Idahoan instant mashed potatoes — 3 pouches (makes about 5 cups prepared)
  • Low-fat cottage cheese — 1 tub (24 oz), blended smooth
  • Frozen corn — 3 bags (16 oz each), about 7½ cups
  • Reduced-fat shredded cheddar — 1 bag (8 oz); each bowl gets 1 Tbsp
  • Heinz chicken gravy — 1 jar (12 oz); each bowl gets 2 Tbsp

Method

  1. Cook the chicken until it hits 165°F, then shred it. Shredded spreads across the bowl and eats bigger than chunks.
  2. Make the instant mash. Blend the cottage cheese smooth and whip it into the hot mash with a splash of hot water until fluffy. That is the base.
  3. Heat the corn. Warm the gravy.
  4. Build each container: ½ cup mash base, ¾ cup corn, 4 oz shredded chicken, 1 Tbsp cheddar, 2 Tbsp gravy over the top.
⚖️ Portioning — how a serving stays honest

Each dish makes 10 portions, 5 per person. The enchilada batch is about 4,800 cal total and the KFC batch about 4,870, so one portion is one tenth of the batch.

  • Stir the finished dish really well first so chicken and rice do not separate.
  • With a kitchen scale: weigh the whole batch, divide by 10, fill each container to that weight. The KFC bowls are built per bowl instead, using the amounts in the recipe.
  • No scale: fill all 10 containers in rounds, one scoop in each, then level them off.
  • Storage: 6 containers per dish in the fridge (Mon–Wed), 4 in the freezer (Thu–Fri). Move frozen ones to the fridge the night before.
