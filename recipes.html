⭐ Mexican Chicken & Rice — the written recipe

The creator's recipe, doubled to make our 10 portions. Cups and pounds, the way we actually cook it.

450 cal · 45P · 47C · 9F per portion

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 2 value packs (7 lb total across both Week 1 dishes; about half goes in here)
  • Dry rice — about 2¼ cups (we used 2 cups our first time and it felt like too little rice, don't short it)
  • Black beans — 1 can (15 oz), drained & rinsed
  • Canned diced tomatoes — 2 cans (14.5 oz each), our addition; not in the creator's written recipe
  • Yellow onions — 2, diced
  • Red bell peppers — 2, diced
  • Limes — 2 (juice + wedges)
  • Fresh coriander / cilantro — chopped, for garnish
  • Chicken seasoning: 4 tsp oregano, 4 tsp paprika, 2 tsp each of garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, salt & black pepper
  • Olive oil — 4 tsp, for cooking
  • About 2½ cups water or chicken stock (the tomato juice counts as liquid)

Method

  1. Cube the chicken and season it all over with the chicken seasoning above and the juice of 2 limes. Rest 10 minutes while you chop the onion and peppers.
  2. Cook the chicken in a little olive oil in a big pot or deep pan, 6–8 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In the same pot, cook the onion and bell pepper until soft.
  4. Add the dry rice, 2 tsp of each seasoning (the same mix as the chicken), and the diced tomatoes. Toast 1–2 minutes, stirring.
  5. Add the water/stock and the black beans. Mix well, cover, and simmer on low 12–15 minutes, stirring halfway so the rice doesn't stick.
  6. Fold the chicken back in with the chopped coriander and a final squeeze of lime. Divide into 10 containers.
